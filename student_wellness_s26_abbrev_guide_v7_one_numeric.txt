Student Wellness S26 Abbreviation Guide / ISLE Notes

This version is designed for ISLE/DataExplorer import. It removes all numeric score columns except wellbeing_index.

Kept as the only quantitative variable:
- wellbeing_index: average of Q1-Q8 well-being items

Removed from the data file:
- all *_score variables
- school_support_index

Common abbreviated response levels:
- SD = Strongly disagree
- D = Disagree
- N = Neither agree nor disagree
- A = Agree
- SA = Strongly agree
- NA = Not applicable

Frequency scale:
- Nvr = Never
- R = Rarely
- S = Sometimes
- O = Often
- VO = Very often

Money stress scale:
- None = Not at all heavy
- Slight = Slightly heavy
- Mod = Moderately heavy
- Very = Very heavy
- Ext = Extremely heavy

ISLE-safe range labels:
- age_group: 17 or younger; 18 or 19; 20 or 21; 22 to 25; 26 or older; Prefer not
- study_hours_outside_class: under 5 hours; 5 to 10 hours; 11 to 19 hours; 20 or more hours
- spring_credits: 1 to 5 credits; 6 to 8 credits; 9 to 11 credits; 12 to 14 credits; 15 or more credits
- paid_work_hours: 0 hours; 1 to 9 hours; 10 to 19 hours; 20 to 29 hours; 30 or more hours

Other common abbreviated categories:
- MENA = Middle Eastern / North African
- Black/African Am. = Black / African / African American
- Arts/creative = Arts and creative fields
- Career/tech = Career/technical fields
- Not conf. = Not confident at all
- Low conf. = Not very confident
- Some conf. = Somewhat confident
- Very conf. = Very confident
- No major = I have not chosen a major or area of study yet
